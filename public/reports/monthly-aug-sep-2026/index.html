09 • OCTOBER ACTION PLAN
Three growth engines

Naikkan utilization tanpa merusak peak-hour value.

01 • Corporate Booking

2–4 blocks / month

Target perusahaan, instansi, bank, oil & gas, kontraktor. Paket 4–12+ jam dengan referee, photographer, mineral water, tournament management.

Objective: duration + ticket size
02 • Monthly Event

1 owned event / month

Event sebagai acquisition engine: participant database → CRM → repeat booking → membership.

Objective: acquisition + repeat
03 • Team Subscription

5–8 recurring teams

Paket 4 / 8 / 12 jam per bulan, fixed weekly slot, priority booking, bundle referee/photographer, reschedule privilege.

Objective: recurring revenue
